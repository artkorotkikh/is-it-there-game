## Limits and remaining acceptance

- Multiplayer, signaling and TURN have not been implemented or tested. Static hosting on the existing ICP Cloud Engine is deployed and verified.
- The three routes remain a compact solo prototype. Scripted timings are optimized test runs; intended **2–5 minute human/cooperative playtime** is not established. Do not treat an automated run as evidence of fun.
- The camera has terrain-height protection and a wider winch view, but no general collision/occlusion avoidance. Distant trees, rocks, posts and mountains are decorative, not complete physical obstacles. All orange-banded anchor trees and Relay ridge roadblocks have colliders. Terrain and its shoulders do have matching rendered/colliding geometry; the character is constrained inside their outer boundary.
- The new held kick rights an accessible overturned bus; getting pinned under an obstacle or leaving recoverable terrain may still require a pause-menu restart. There is no checkpoint persistence, host migration or reconnection.
- Player pushing/dragging, a full animation asset pipeline, simulated vehicle damage and day/night timing are deferred. The requested procedural walking/idle animation and cosmetic wear are implemented.
- Headless browser tests use software WebGL and do not establish a performance target for player hardware. Chrome phone emulation is covered; actual iOS/Android hardware, Safari, Firefox and adverse real-network conditions remain unverified.
- The local winch can be operated by the lone player; two-player seat/cable ownership validation belongs to the networking stage.
- Cable terrain contacts are frictionless and quasi-static. Slack is a visual shape fitted to paid-out length; it has no independent inertia, breakage, or collision/wrapping around arbitrary obstacles. This is not a full segmented rope simulation.
- Module handles, licence tag and roof hatch are visual geometry; hand articulation and the door hinge are not simulated bodies. Chassis mass stays a fixed loaded envelope. There is no fuel consumption, refilling, generic inventory or real ICP-balance interaction.

## Not implemented

Second player, WebRTC, room/signaling backend, TURN credentials, and real-network cooperative playtesting. The records backend is separate from those deferred features.
